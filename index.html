The series

The Hub Series ranks paddlers across the state championships and the Australian Age Nationals. Each race's official results decide the placings in C1M, K1M, C1W and K1W. Those placings are turned into points.

Race points

In each class at each race, the winner gets 1000 points and every place after that gets an equal share less:

points = 1000 − (place − 1) × 1000 ÷ N, rounded to the nearest whole number (halves round up).

  • N is the number of paddlers placed in the class at that race, with a minimum of 5. Small fields don't give away big gaps for single places. With 2 paddlers, points are 1000 and 800. With 8 paddlers they are 1000, 875, 750, 625, 500, 375, 250 and 125.
  • Missing a race scores 0 for that race.

Class standings

  • Your total in a class is the sum of your points from every race in that class. Every race counts.
  • Paddlers with the same total share the rank, shown as “=13”. The next rank is skipped (=13, =13, 15).

Combined standings

The women's (C1W + K1W) and men's (C1M + K1M) combined standings average the two class totals:

Avg = (C1 total + K1 total) ÷ 2, rounded to the nearest whole number (halves round up).

If you race only one class, the other counts as 0, so paddling both classes is rewarded.
